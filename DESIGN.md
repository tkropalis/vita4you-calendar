# Design System: Πρόγραμμα · Vita4you Τσιμισκή

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Native Agenda"**

Πρόγραμμα is the category standard played straight: a staff-scheduling app with the content discipline of Deputy and When I Work and the calm, native polish of Apple Calendar on iOS. The shared rota spreadsheet is rebuilt as one person's week, laid out as an agenda list with one row per day. It is not a spreadsheet and not a calendar grid of floating blocks. The system refuses metaphor, decoration and quirk. Trust comes from precision: exact times in tabular numerals, the period of every shift legible at a glance, and a freshness clock beside the refresh control.

The material is iOS grouped lists. A cool, faintly tinted ground holds near-white grouped sections with hairline separators. One blue accent does all the interactive work: selection, today, primary actions, links and focus. The only other colour in the system is data. Shift chips are soft-tinted by period: amber for morning, violet for afternoon, slate with a moon for duty and night, green for ρεπό and stone for άδεια. Type is the platform system face, with hierarchy built from size and weight steps.

The scene is a phone held in one hand between shifts, so the page is light by default and follows the device into dark mode automatically, with every colour token and the float shadow redefined for the dark scheme. Density is moderate. Rows are at least 52px, primary controls are at least 44px, and the week range is the largest type on the page. The product deliberately carries no Vita4you logo and no Vita4you brand colours. It has its own look.

**Key Characteristics:**
- iOS grouped-list surfaces: a tinted ground, near-white sections, 1px hairlines and 16px corners.
- One blue accent for selection, today and actions, and nothing else.
- Shift chips tinted by period as soft-fill and dark-ink pairs from one hue.
- The platform system face, with tabular numerals for every time and date.
- Lucide stroke icons at 18px, stroke width 2, in the colour of the text around them.
- Flat surfaces. Shadows appear only on a selected thumb and on the floating toast.
- Light by scene, with full dark-mode parity through `prefers-color-scheme`.

## Colors

The palette is restrained: cool tinted neutrals, one calendar blue for interaction, and five soft period tints that exist only to classify shifts. All values are authored in OKLCH in `app/globals.css`. Light values sit on `:root`, and each `-dark` key is the same role redefined inside `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`. Never use a `-dark` value in the light scheme.

### Primary
- **Calendar Blue** (`accent`, oklch(51.5% 0.19 259)): the one interactive colour. It fills primary buttons and today's date circle, colours icon buttons, the refresh control, links and the focus ring, and marks the selected person in the team view. In dark mode it lifts to oklch(70% 0.15 256) and is paired with near-black ink.
- **Pressed Blue** (`accent-hover`, oklch(46% 0.19 259)): the hover fill of primary buttons.
- **Blue Wash** (`accent-soft`, oklch(93.8% 0.032 259)): the soft accent field. It backs initial avatars, the "Σήμερα" pill, icon-button hover, the selected row of the team matrix and text selection. Mixed with `surface`, it washes today's row: 55% wash in the agenda and summary, 45% wash in the team matrix's today column.
- **Wash Ink** (`accent-soft-text`, oklch(42% 0.17 259)): the text colour used on Blue Wash. It sets avatar initials, today's weekday label and the selected name in the matrix.
- **On Blue** (`on-accent`, oklch(99% 0.003 265)): text and icons on Calendar Blue.

### Shift Periods
A categorical data palette, not a set of accents. Each period is a pair: a light fill (`*-bg`) and a dark ink of the same hue family (`*-fg`), each pair around 7:1 or better in both schemes.
- **Morning Amber** (`morning-bg` / `morning-fg`): πρωινή shifts.
- **Afternoon Violet** (`afternoon-bg` / `afternoon-fg`): απογευματινή shifts.
- **Duty Slate** (`duty-bg` / `duty-fg`): εφημερία and ολονυχτία evening and night duty. Full-size duty chips carry the lucide Moon icon.
- **Rest Green** (`off-bg` / `off-fg`): ρεπό. `off-fg` also colours the ρεπό date in the week summary.
- **Leave Stone** (`leave-bg` / `leave-fg`): άδεια and γονική άδεια.

### Feedback
- **Alert Red** (`danger`, oklch(52% 0.19 27)): fill of the error toast in the light scheme.
- **Alert Wash** (`danger-soft`, oklch(95% 0.03 27)): the "sheet unreachable" banner, and the error toast in the dark scheme.

### Neutral
- **Cool Ground** (`bg`, oklch(96.6% 0.005 265)): the page ground. The browser `theme-color` matches it in both schemes.
- **Card White** (`surface`, oklch(99.6% 0.002 265)): grouped sections, the person picker, secondary buttons and the selected segment.
- **Sunken Track** (`surface-sunken`, oklch(94.4% 0.006 265)): recessed fields such as the segmented-control track, the day-picker track, name pills, notices and the empty avatar.
- **Hover Tint** (`surface-hover`, oklch(95.6% 0.006 265)): hover on secondary buttons and the press state of list rows.
- **Hairline** (`hairline`, oklch(89.5% 0.007 265)): section borders, row separators and the dashed separator between two entries on one day.
- **Strong Hairline** (`hairline-strong`, oklch(83% 0.009 265)): borders that must read as controls (secondary buttons, tags, the dashed "Χωρίς ώρα" chip, picker hover) and the matrix scrollbar thumb.
- **Ink** (`text`, oklch(23% 0.022 265)): primary text. In inverted form it is the neutral toast fill.
- **Secondary Ink** (`text-2`, oklch(46% 0.02 265)): metadata, subtitles, row labels in the summary, unselected segments and footnotes.
- **Tertiary Ink** (`text-3`, oklch(55% 0.016 265)): past days, empty matrix cells, list chevrons and disabled icon buttons. Use it on Card White only.

### Named Rules
**The One Blue Rule.** Blue means "you, today, or tap here". It marks selection, today, primary actions, links and focus. It never colours a shift, and a period tint never marks a control.

**The Period Pair Rule.** Every shift period is a soft fill with dark ink from the same hue (`*-bg` with `*-fg`). The chip always carries words or times as well as colour: the colour speeds the scan, and the text carries the meaning.

**The Tinted Neutral Rule.** Every neutral sits on hue 265 at low chroma. Nothing is pure white or pure black, in either scheme.

## Typography

**Display Font:** none. The system has no separate display face.
**Body Font:** the platform system face (`-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, SF Pro Text, Segoe UI, Roboto, Noto Sans, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif).

**Character:** the native voice of the phone it runs on. That is SF Pro on iPhone and Mac and Roboto or Noto Sans on Android, all with full Greek coverage including accented capitals. The face is plain on purpose: the content is the schedule, and the type should feel like the operating system's own calendar. The base size is 15px (0.9375rem). The scale steps are 13, 15, 17, 22 and 28px, plus a 12px caption size.

### Hierarchy
- **Headline** (700, 1.75rem / 28px, line-height 1.15, -0.02em, tabular): the week range ("28 Σεπ – 4 Οκτ"). It is the largest text on the page and the heading of the week.
- **Day Number** (600, 1.375rem / 22px, line-height 1, tabular): the date numeral in the agenda's date column, centred in a 38px circle. The load-error heading also uses this size.
- **Title** (700, 1.0625rem / 17px, line-height 1.2, -0.01em): the app-bar title "Πρόγραμμα" and empty-state titles.
- **Chip** (650, 1.0625rem / 17px, -0.005em, tabular): shift times inside full-size chips ("08:00 – 16:00").
- **Person Name** (600, 1.0625rem / 17px): the selected name in the picker and the rows of the first-visit name list.
- **Body** (400, 0.9375rem / 15px, line-height 1.45): running text and summary values. Empty-state copy is held to 52ch and footnotes to 72ch.
- **Button** (600, 0.9375rem / 15px): button labels and the "Ανανέωση" refresh label.
- **Label** (600, 0.8125rem / 13px): weekday abbreviations, segmented options, compact chips, matrix column heads and the "Σήμερα" pill.
- **Meta** (500, 0.8125rem / 13px): period and duration metadata ("Πρωινή · 8 ώρες"), the relative-week line, group captions and the toast text.
- **Footnote** (400, 0.8125rem / 13px, line-height 1.45): the app-bar subtitle, summary asides, calendar hints, data notes and banners.
- **Caption** (600, 0.75rem / 12px, tabular where numeric): the freshness clock ("έλεγχος 22:46"), tags, the "Άλλαξε" badge (700) and day-picker weekday labels.

### Named Rules
**The Platform Voice Rule.** One family, the platform system face, and no webfont. Hierarchy comes from the five size steps and from weight (400 to 700), never from a second family.

**The Tabular Time Rule.** Times, date numbers, week ranges and the freshness clock are set in tabular numerals, so that columns of times line up.

## Layout

The layout is phone-first, in a single column inside a 16px gutter. The gutter grows to the safe-area inset where the device has one. The app shell is capped at 1120px and centred. The bottom padding is 96px plus the safe-area inset, which leaves room for the toast.

Spacing follows a 2px-granular rhythm in the iOS manner. The working steps are 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20 and 24px. 8 and 12 are the most common, 16 is the gutter and the gap between major blocks, and 24 separates column groups and the footer. Rows within grouped sections are at least 52px tall, with 10 to 16px of padding.

Responsive behaviour:
- **From 760px (viewport):** the person picker (up to 360px) and the view switch (up to 320px) sit side by side, aligned to their bottom edge. The week navigator centres its range between the two arrows with a 16px gap.
- **My week, from an 820px container:** the agenda takes the flexible column and the summary group plus calendar actions take a 300px column on the right, with a 24px gap. The week navigator re-centres over the agenda.
- **Team, from a 700px container:** a full matrix appears, with a sticky 170px name column and seven day columns. It has a 720px minimum width and scrolls horizontally inside its section. A colour legend made of compact chips sits above it. Below 700px, the team view is a seven-day picker track over grouped period sections that list the names on that shift.

### Named Rules
**The Grouped List Rule.** Content lives in grouped sections on the tinted ground: a Card White fill, a 1px hairline border, 16px corners, and hairline separators between rows. Sections never nest inside other sections.

**The Agenda Rule.** One person's week is a vertical agenda, with one row per day from Monday to Sunday, the date column on the left and the entries on the right. The matrix belongs to the team view only.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat and tonal. Depth comes from three tonal layers: the Cool Ground, Card White sections above it, and the Sunken Track recessed below it. Hairlines draw the edges. Resting surfaces carry no shadow. Shadows are short and soft, tinted with the ink hue rather than black, and they appear in exactly three places.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Thumb** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px oklch(25% 0.03 265 / 0.14), 0 1px 1px oklch(25% 0.03 265 / 0.06)`): the selected option lifting out of the segmented-control track.
- **Day Thumb** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px oklch(25% 0.03 265 / 0.14)`): the chosen day lifting out of the day-picker track.
- **Float** (`--shadow-float`: `0 8px 28px -6px oklch(25% 0.03 265 / 0.22), 0 2px 6px oklch(25% 0.03 265 / 0.08)`; dark `0 10px 30px -6px oklch(5% 0.01 265 / 0.6), 0 2px 6px oklch(5% 0.01 265 / 0.4)`): the toast floating over content.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Ground Rule.** Resting surfaces carry no shadow. A shadow means something sits above a track (the selected segment, the chosen day) or floats over content (the toast).

## Shapes

The form language is soft rounded rectangles in four sizes, plus pills and circles. Grouped sections, the person picker, the day-picker track, notices and toasts use 16px corners. Buttons, banners, day-picker cells and the refresh control use 12px. The focus ring and toast actions use 8px. Shift chips use 9px, and so do segmented options, whose track is 11px around a 3px inset. Compact matrix chips use 7px. Tags, the "Σήμερα" pill, the change badge and name pills are full pills (999px).

Corners are concentric where one shape sits inside another: the day-picker track (16px) holds 12px cells inside a 4px inset. Borders are always 1px. A solid border marks a container or control. A dashed border marks something secondary or unresolved, such as the separator between two entries on the same day or the "Χωρίς ώρα" chip, which stands for an entry with no time in the sheet.

### Named Rules
**The Circle Rule.** Circles are reserved for people (initial avatars), date numbers and icon-only buttons. Everything else is a rounded rectangle or a pill.

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet, native and full-width on phones in the calendar-actions group.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (12px), at least 44px tall, 16px horizontal padding, an 8px gap to a leading 18px lucide icon.
- **Primary:** Calendar Blue fill with On Blue text, 15px at weight 600 ("Προσθήκη στο ημερολόγιο").
- **Secondary:** a Card White fill with a 1px Strong Hairline border and Ink text ("Συνδρομή που ενημερώνεται").
- **Hover / Focus / Active:** hover is applied only on devices that support hover. Primary shifts to Pressed Blue, and secondary shifts to Hover Tint. Pressing scales the button to 0.98. Transitions run 160ms on the shared ease-out curve. Focus is a 2px Calendar Blue outline at a 2px offset. Disabled buttons drop to 45% opacity.
- **Icon button:** a 44px circle with Calendar Blue icons and no fill. On hover it takes the Blue Wash. When disabled it turns Tertiary Ink and is never washed. The week arrows use this button.
- **Refresh control:** a 44px-tall text button in Calendar Blue with a 12px radius. It stacks the "Ανανέωση" label over a tabular caption reading "έλεγχος HH:MM", and its icon spins (900ms, linear) while checking.
- **Link pill:** "Σήμερα" appears as a Blue Wash pill with Wash Ink text when you leave the current week.

### Chips

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every shift, ρεπό and άδεια in a period chip using its `*-bg` / `*-fg` pair, with the full time ("08:00 – 16:00") in the personal view and the compact form ("08–16") in the matrix.
- **Do** add the lucide Moon icon (15px, stroke 2.25) to full-size duty chips, so that night duty does not depend on colour alone.
- **Do** set every time, date number and week range in tabular numerals.
- **Do** mark today with Calendar Blue: the filled 38px date circle and the 55% Blue Wash on its row.
- **Do** place new content in grouped sections: Card White, 1px Hairline, 16px corners, hairline row separators and rows of at least 52px.
- **Do** keep primary controls at least 44px tall and reachable with a thumb.
- **Do** draw icons from lucide only, at 18px with stroke 2 in `currentColor` (14px inline, 16px in notes).
- **Do** theme the browser surfaces from the palette: Blue Wash text selection, a 2px Calendar Blue focus ring at a 2px offset, links underlined at a 0.2em offset with 1px thickness, and a thin Strong Hairline scrollbar.
- **Do** define every new colour twice, once on `:root` and once in the `prefers-color-scheme: dark` block, keeping its role.
- **Do** animate with the shared ease-out curve (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) at 160 to 260ms, and let `prefers-reduced-motion` collapse all motion.
- **Do** keep Tertiary Ink on Card White. On the Cool Ground it falls below 4.5:1, so it appears there only as a disabled state.

### Don't:
- **Don't** colour a shift with Calendar Blue, and don't use a period tint on a button, link or selection.
- **Don't** use pure white or pure black. Neutrals stay on hue 265.
- **Don't** load a webfont or add a second type family.
- **Don't** saturate the period fills. They stay soft (around 93% lightness in light mode and 30 to 33% in dark mode) with dark ink.
- **Don't** lay out one person's week as a time grid of floating blocks or as a spreadsheet. It is an agenda, one row per day.
- **Don't** add decoration, metaphor, illustration or gradients. Trust comes from precision.
- **Don't** use the Vita4you logo or Vita4you brand colours. The product has its own look.
- **Don't** put a shadow on a resting section or card.
- **Don't** nest a grouped section inside another grouped section.
